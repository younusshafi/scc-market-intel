Financial Performance
The Company has demonstrated resilience and a commitment to growth during the period ending September 2024. The parent company achieved an
impressive revenue of OMR 196.5 million, representing an 11.2% increase compared to the same period in 2023. This performance highlights our continued
dedication to executing strategic projects and securing a strong market presence.
Despite the reported net loss of OMR 1.0 million, our significant revenue growth signals a positive trend towards operational recovery. Additionally, the
substantial expansion of our order book to a record OMR 890 million, up from OMR 589 million in the previous year, underscores our strategic success in
securing high-value contracts, particularly within the Oil & Gas and Infrastructure sectors. These achievements provide a strong foundation for future financial
stability and growth.
Our subsidiary companies, including Aspire Readymix, Aspire Projects, and Al Khalij Heavy Equipment, have shown progressive operational improvements.

While these subsidiaries reported a combined loss of OMR 0.513 million for the period, our focused turnaround strategies are driving gradual performance
enhancements that align with our long-term objectives. Our associate company in Kuwait reported a loss of RO 0.289 million (Galfar Share) for the period
ended Sep 2024. The Parent Company continues to make-up 93% of the entire consolidated business.
Your company is determined to improve its subsidiaries and associates’ performance and has put in place the strategic plans and measures to
overcome the associated challenges to further improve the business with full support from the parent company.
Outlook for the Future
Looking ahead, Galfar is poised to capitalize on its strengthened position within the industry. Our strategic plan is geared towards sustainable growth and
excellence in project delivery. The expansion of Oman’s economy, driven by increased government and private sector investments, provides a fertile
environment for our continued success. We are confident that our robust order book and active participation in development projects will maintain momentum
and reinforce our market leadership.
In line with Oman Vision 2040, we anticipate further collaboration with the government and private sector for Public-Private Partnership (PPP) projects,
ensuring our role as a key contributor to national growth. The pipeline of projects in sectors such as education, health, transportation, and technology continues
to grow, and we are prepared to seize these opportunities to drive our revenue and market presence.
Our commitment to innovation and sustainability positions Galfar as a forward-thinking leader, aligning with Oman’s net-zero emissions target by 2050. By
integrating technology and efficiency measures, we aim to enhance our project execution and contribute to a more sustainable future.
With a focus on diversification into new markets, including Construction Technology and Energy, we are establishing pathways for long-term resilience. Our
strategic priorities of restructuring, cost efficiency, financial optimization, and stakeholder engagement remain central to our approach.
Galfar’s unwavering dedication to employee development, as demonstrated by our 4,400-strong Omani workforce, ensures that we have the talent and
capability to meet the challenges of the future. Through continued training and development initiatives, we are preparing our workforce to contribute effectively
to the evolving needs of the industry.
As we build on our strong foundation, we remain committed to maintaining our legacy as a flagship company in Oman’s engineering and contracting sector.
With strategic focus, dedicated execution, and an eye on future opportunities, Galfar is well-positioned to deliver enhanced financial performance and
shareholder value in the years to come.
