Financial and Operational Highlights
Despite a challenging and evolving market environment, Galfar demonstrated resilience, maintaining strong revenue levels. The Parent Company recorded revenues of
OMR 59.9 million, marginally lower than the prior year, consistent with the scope and timing of project execution. This underscores Galfar’s ability to consistently secure
and deliver strategic projects, reinforcing its long-standing and competitive market position.
Notably, the company’s order book remained robust at OMR 904 million despite the absence of major new awards in quarter one 2025. This healthy backlog solidifies
Galfar’s future revenue visibility and reflects successful positioning in critical sectors such as Oil & Gas and Infrastructure.
While the Group reported a net loss of OMR 0.680 million for the quarter, this performance was influenced by external market pressures including rise in material and
service prices, in addition to specific project challenges. However, the underlying operational momentum, focus on project delivery excellence and revenue stability provide
a strong foundation for future profitability improvements.
Subsidiary companies, particularly Aspire Ready-Mix, Aspire Projects, and Al Khalij Heavy Equipment, continue to progress towards improved performance,
collectively delivering a modest profit of OMR 0.033 million. Their ongoing turnaround efforts are expected to contribute more meaningfully to the Group's bottom line
going forward.
The Parent Company continues to account for approximately 90% of the Group’s consolidated revenue, maintaining its critical role within the Group’s performance
structure.

Future Outlook
Looking ahead, Galfar is sharply focused on:
Strengthening profitability
·
· Driving operational efficiencies
· Enhancing cash flow management
· Optimizing working capital
· Accelerating innovation and digital transformation
Our revitalized strategic roadmap emphasizes People and Organizational Culture, Operational Excellence, Financial Resilience, Sustainability & Diversification,
Technology & Innovation
— pillars that will steer us toward achieving sustained shareholder value and industry leadership.
The broader economic landscape in Oman, supported by strong government initiatives and private sector investments, presents significant growth opportunities. Galfar is
well-positioned to capitalize on these through active participation in potential projects across various sectors, aligned with Oman Vision 2040.
Our expanding project pipeline across infrastructure, energy, utilities, and transportation
sectors strengthens our optimism for sustainable revenue and margin growth.
Moreover, Galfar remains committed to supporting Oman’s 2050 net-zero emissions target by prioritizing innovation, digital transformation, and sustainable business
practices. Our diversification strategy extends into emerging sectors such as Construction Technology and Renewable Energy, complementing our traditional strengths.
At the heart of Galfar’s success is its talented workforce, notably the over 4,200 Omani employees. We remain deeply committed to fostering talent, investing in upskilling
initiatives, and building a high-performance, values-driven culture to ensure we are future-ready.
With a clear strategic vision, operational discipline, and a forward-looking approach, Galfar is poised to deliver improved financial performance, enhanced shareholder
returns, and long-term sustainable growth.
